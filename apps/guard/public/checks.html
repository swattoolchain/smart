Every check we run

Store Guard tests your store the way a customer uses it, then checks the things customers feel but rarely report. Live checks run today; Coming ones are in development.

Shopping journey (functional)

Store is reachableLiveHomepage loads, unlocks password-protected stores with your storefront password, and reports downtime with the HTTP status.
Collection page shows productsLiveOpens a real collection and confirms the product grid renders.
Search returns productsLiveSearches a word taken from one of your product titles and expects results.
Product page worksLiveOpens an in-stock product and confirms a price is visible.
Variant selectionLiveOpens a second variant and confirms the product form selects it, so customers buy what they picked.
Add to cartLiveClicks your real Add to cart button and confirms the item reaches the cart. If it fails, we test the cart directly to tell you whether your theme or an app broke the button.
CartLiveConfirms the cart page lists the item and a total.
Checkout opensLiveClicks Check out and confirms the checkout contact form appears, then stops. No payment details, no orders. The cart is emptied afterwards.
Discount codesComingApplies a code you choose and confirms the total changes.
Shipping and pincode serviceabilityComingChecks delivery estimates or pincode checkers return results for your key regions.
Customer login and order historyComingLogs into a test account and opens order history.

Regression

Visual changesLiveScreenshots of the homepage and a product page on desktop and mobile, compared with the look you approved. Animations are frozen so only real changes count.
Daily full check plus journeys every 3 hoursLiveMonitoring runs on a schedule, and failing stores are re-checked every 30 minutes until they recover.
Failures confirmed before alertingLiveA newly failing critical check is re-tested a minute later. You get alerts only when something breaks or recovers, never the same problem twice.
Run tests when your theme is publishedComingTriggered automatically by theme changes through the Shopify app.
Test an unpublished theme before it goes liveComingRun the suite against a preview theme before you publish.

Speed and stability

Mobile load speedLiveLargest content paint on homepage and product page at phone size: good under 2.5s, poor over 4s.
Slow third-party appsLiveNames the outside scripts that take longest to load, so you know which app to question.
Layout stabilityLiveMeasures content jumping while loading, the cause of mis-taps.
Page weight and requestsLiveFlags pages that are heavy on mobile data.

Errors, links and security hygiene

JavaScript errorsLiveCaptures script errors during the shopping journey. Apps and custom code often break buttons silently.
Broken linksLiveChecks links on your homepage, menus and footer.
Insecure contentLiveFinds resources still loaded over http:// that browsers may block.

SEO and accessibility

Hidden from GoogleLiveCatches a noindex tag accidentally left on your homepage or product pages.
SEO basicsLiveTitles, descriptions, H1 headings, canonical links and social share images.
AccessibilityLiveAutomated WCAG 2 A/AA checks: contrast, labels, names for buttons and form fields.
Image descriptionsLiveImages missing alt text, which matters for screen readers and image search.

Catalog and trust

Catalog completenessLiveProducts priced at zero, without images, with empty descriptions, or mostly sold out.
Store policiesLivePrivacy, refund, terms and shipping policies that payment providers and ad platforms check for.
Order confirmation emails, WhatsApp and SMSComingAlerts when order messages fail to reach customers, including COD confirmations.
